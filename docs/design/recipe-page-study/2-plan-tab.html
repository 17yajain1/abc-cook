Sabudana Khichdi
2 hr 40 min
NOW · 5 MIN
Rinse and soak 5 min

Rinse 1 cup sabudana until the water runs clear. Cover with water just to the top of the pearls.

Let it soak 2 hr
until a pearl squashes easily between two fingers
Meanwhile, get ahead
your hands are free · 20 min of prep fits in the 2 hr
START WITH THIS
Roast the peanuts5 min
Dry-roast ¼ cup raw peanuts until they crackle and smell nutty.
Crush the peanuts5 min
Make the green chutney3 min
Boil and cube the potatoes5 min
Chop chillies and coriander2 min
THEN · ~30 MIN AT THE STOVE
You'll need
soaked sabudana 3 tbsp ghee ½ tsp cumin 12 curry leaves potatoes +4 more
  1. Coat the sabudana3 min

    Drain well. Toss with the crushed peanuts and sendha namak so every pearl is coated.

  2. Temper3 min

    Heat 3 tbsp ghee. Add ½ tsp cumin, 12 curry leaves and the chillies.

  3. Add potatoes, then sabudana6 min

    Stir the potatoes through, then fold in the sabudana gently on low.

  4. Cook until clear8 min

    Stir now and then, until the pearls turn translucent.

  5. Finish5 min

    Squeeze in lemon juice, scatter coriander and mix.
