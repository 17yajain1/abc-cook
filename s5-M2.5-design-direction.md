## Step 0 — what the real screens showed

The roadmap's precondition for this milestone is reacting to real screens rather than to
code, so the first thing was booting both servers and walking all five fixtures at 390px.
Three findings were not surface, and two of them are still open on purpose.

1. **There is no time axis anywhere, and the product is about time.** Homemade Donuts made
   it undeniable: `First rise · 60 min` rendered identically to `Bloom yeast · 4 min`
   directly above it. Every duration in the app was a *text label*, never a *dimension*.
2. **The wait window assumes its host is hot.** Donuts rendered `⚡ WHILE THIS COOKS` and
   `🔥 60 min cooking` over dough proving at room temperature. Kadai Paneer hid this
   because its host genuinely is a pan.
3. **The window's numbers don't reconcile.** Chicken Biryani's second window read
   `2 min prep`, `fits in 12 min`, `Fits with 7 min to spare` — and 2 + 7 ≠ 12. The
   header's denominator is the host's `duration_typical`; the footer's slack is
   `window.slack_min` against the window's real capacity after gating.

Surface findings that drove the retone: the stage ramp was two palettes wearing one coat
(spice tones beside Tailwind violet-400 and a generic sky blue); `--stage-0 #f2b134` was
visually identical to `--color-saffron #e8a020`, so the documented "saffron is not in the
ramp" reservation existed only in prose; the header never scrolled and ate 270–320px of an
844px screen; the header gradient ended in a hard horizontal seam; the product's own claim
("saves you 46 min") was set as a caption; capacity chips carried no magnitude, so a
75%-full window and a 5%-full one rendered identically; and Maggi — parallelism stripped
out — was visually indistinguishable from ReciMe.

Two spec violations surfaced that were not design opinions:

- **`PlanScreen`'s CTA escaped the 390px column.** `absolute` inside a root with no
  `position`, so it resolved against the viewport. Invisible at exactly 390px, which is
  why M2 shipped it. Fixed by adding `relative`.
- **`CLAUDE.md` describes Figma frames as versioned v1→v4.** The Make file is on its own
  "Version 8" counter with a single design in it, and Make's own assistant says so in the
  owner's chat log. Corrected.

**The legend mockup referenced by `GRAPH_VIEW.md` §5 does not exist.** Not in the working
tree, not in any commit across all history, and the only tracked images are the favicon,
`icons.svg` and `hero.png`. The Map is greenfield.

## The direction

Three were proposed — **Timetable**, **Score** (musical notation), **Hob** (refine the
existing dark palette). The owner chose Timetable, and the reasoning is worth keeping: it
derives the visual language from the core product concept rather than applying a mood to
it. Score was the more distinctive artifact and the worse bet against a civilian
five-second test; Hob was the safe option that would most likely have failed M2.5's own
exit criterion.

> **A cooking plan is a timetable.** Rail schedules solved this exact display problem —
> several things moving at once, against one clock, where the reader needs *when* and
> *what's concurrent* in a glance.

Three principles: time is measured not labelled; one signal colour; **absence has a
shape** — unattended work is drawn hollow, and the empty space inside the mark *is* the
room you have.

## Decisions locked

| # | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Light ground.** paper `#EFEEEA`, true black ink, one rationed signal red `#C42F16`. | Owner explicitly accepted the light flip as part of the direction, not something to soften. Also the legible choice in a bright kitchen. |
| 2 | **True black, not tinted near-black.** | `#111`/`#0B0B0B` standing in for black is a recognised generated-design tell. A printed schedule uses black ink. |
| 3 | **Green retired.** "All this prep fits" is a fact, not an achievement. | A second accent dilutes principle 2. M3 will need a completion mark; that is M3's call and must not resurrect a general-purpose green. |
| 4 | **Stage identity kept but re-derived** — six equal-value, low-chroma tints, none red, drawn as a 3px lane rule. | Owner's constraint: keep identity, kill the rainbow, signal stays strongest. |
| 5 | **Stage rail stays ordinal**, treatment goes quiet. | Owner's call: ordinal answers "where am I", the time axis answers "when". Distinct jobs; don't duplicate temporal information into the rail. |
| 6 | **Radius encodes measurement vs control** — 0 / 2px / 50%. **No shadow anywhere.** | Resolves the "one radius and one shadow on everything" open item as a *rule*, not a value. |
| 7 | **ALL-CAPS eyebrows and middle-dot meta retired.** Durations move to a right-aligned tabular column. | Both were named tells. The column is the timetable's own device and removes most dots for free. |
| 8 | **Arrows only where they encode a dependency.** | So the chip `›`, row `›` and picker `›` go; the collapsed stage summary's `→` stays, because there it means "then" and a collapsed card has no vertical axis to carry it. |
| 9 | **Archivo variable, self-hosted, 49KB, both axes.** | See below. |
| 10 | **A node is a bar on a time axis, not a card — but that grammar is the Map's.** The Plan view stays rows. | `GRAPH_VIEW.md` §2 gives the Plan a different job and §6 says spend the boldness on the Map and nowhere else. |
| 11 | **`color-scheme: light` is required, not cosmetic.** | Without it Chrome's auto-dark inverts the whole page and destroys contrast ratios chosen against paper. The M2 dark ground hid this. |

### The webfont rule, kept in intent and relaxed in mechanism

`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` banned webfonts because they cost "a render blocking round trip" — a
rule written against a Google Fonts `<link>`. Self-hosting from our own origin, preloaded,
with `font-display: optional`, has no blocking round trip and no layout shift, so the
rule's *intent* survives.

The budget set in the brief was ≤55KB with an explicit fallback: if the two-axis file
busts it, drop the width axis and ship two static cuts. Upstream's latin file is **88KB**.
Clamping the axes to the range the type scale actually uses (`wght` 300–700, `wdth`
88–112) and cutting the charset to what the product renders brings it to **50,192 bytes
with both axes intact** — so the width axis, which carries the signage voice, survived.
`apps/web/scripts/subset-font.py` reproduces it and is deliberately **not** wired into
`make`: the output is committed so the normal build needs neither Python nor network.

### Contrast was checked, not assumed — and the first draft failed

`ink-3` at `#8A877E` was 3.1:1 and signal at `#D8371B` was 4.0:1 against paper. Both fail
AA for normal text, and the failure mode is silent: they look fine on a bright desk
monitor and disappear on a 13px duration read at arm's length in a kitchen. Corrected to
`#6E6B62` (4.6:1) and `#C42F16` (4.8:1) before any component was touched.

### The Map study

`docs/design/map-study-kadai-paneer.html`. Hand-placed static SVG, hardcoded geometry, in
`docs/` and not on any code path — it cannot become production by accident. Every time is
read from the kadai-paneer fixture; nothing invented. Vertical scale is
`34*sqrt(minutes)` per `GRAPH_VIEW.md` §4.4, so 12 min is 231px and 2 min is 48px.

What it demonstrates: a real time gutter in minutes; the critical path as a continuous
signal rail that stays unbroken through 8→10 where the mainline is *blocked* waiting on
the tomatoes; solid fill for attended work against one hollow bar for the 12-minute
unattended cook; the three prep tasks drawn at exactly that bar's vertical extent, so
simultaneity is stated by shared position and needs no connector at all; a heavy merge
stroke where two lanes arrive at one instant; and a thin subordinate rejoin. No
arrowheads — on a time axis, down is later — and no legend.

Two honest limitations, both recorded in the file:

- **Long crossing edges are not solved.** `cube_paneer` feeds `add_paneer` across the
  graph. Routed round the margin it read as a *box outline* rather than a connector —
  exactly the ambiguity the no-legend rule exists to prevent. The study draws the binding
  merge and rejoin only. M2.75's layout engine draws every edge and will need a real
  routing strategy; this study does not pretend to have one.
- **The hollow bar is deliberately unlabelled.** Captioning it "cooks without you" was
  written and then removed: it would pass the five-second test by cheating. Whether the
  mark reads on its own is precisely what the test is for.
