## Checkpoints

### B1 — the caption, rendered in the real Direction 3 field

Not judged as isolated strings (owner's instruction) — rendered in composition across all
five host cases:

| Host | `attention` | Numeral | Caption | True? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| kadai `cook_tomato_base` | periodic | 12 | minutes, checking now and then | ✓ |
| biryani `cook_chicken` | periodic | 12 | minutes, checking now and then | ✓ |
| biryani `soak_rice` | unattended | 20 | minutes hands off | ✓ (cold soak) |
| donuts `first_rise` | unattended | 60 | minutes hands off | ✓ (room-temp dough) |
| shortcake `bake_shortcakes` | unattended | 18 | minutes hands off | ✓ (hot oven, cook is still free) |

Owner accepted the strings as starting points. Flagged for later: whether "checking now
and then" holds for a long `periodic` simmer — both fixture `periodic` hosts happen to be
12 min, so there's no evidence either way. Recorded as calibration.

### B2 — warmth-floor checkpoint (Kadai Paneer, 390×844)

**First render failed the floor at one component**: the wait-window field on
`--color-paper-sunk` (ΔE 4.3) was the faintest region on the screen — the signature
component had inverted from most-present (M2) to least-present. Category A finding,
reported, stopped.

**Corrected render (`--color-field` #E7DCC3) clears the floor.** The field reads
unmistakably as a warm, grounded, printed slab — a place the plan steps onto. The rest of
the screen was already clearing the floor (confident promise line, signal CTA, the 44px
numeral as a real focal moment).

Three fixes landed before the corrected render: CTA to signal/inert; Shortcake host-label
strengthened to black (it was rendering, just faint); two distinct stray-ordinal bugs
(donuts absorb-mode layout; a z-index regression putting an ordinal over the CTA).
